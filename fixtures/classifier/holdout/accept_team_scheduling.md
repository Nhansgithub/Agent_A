# final_PRD_Team Scheduling

## Problem
Shift managers build weekly rotas in a spreadsheet and share them over chat. Staff miss updates,
double-bookings slip through, and there is no record of who agreed to cover what.

## Solution
A scheduling tool where managers draft a weekly rota, publish it, and staff confirm their shifts.
Conflicts (double-booking, exceeding contracted hours) are flagged before publishing.

## Requirements
- Draft a weekly rota by dragging staff onto shifts.
- Flag conflicts: overlapping shifts, hours over contract, missing coverage.
- Publish a rota, notifying affected staff.
- Let staff confirm, or request a swap that a manager approves.
- Keep an audit trail of who was scheduled and who confirmed.

## Scope
In scope: drafting, conflict flags, publishing, confirmations, swaps. Out of scope: payroll
integration, holiday accrual.
