# final_PRD_Dark Mode

## Problem
Users working in low light report eye strain; dark mode is our single most-requested setting.

## Solution
A theme toggle offering Light, Dark, and System. The choice persists per user and applies across web
and mobile.

## Requirements
- A toggle in Settings with Light / Dark / System options.
- System follows the OS preference and updates live when it changes.
- The choice persists across sessions and devices.
- All screens and components are legible in both themes (WCAG AA contrast).

## Scope
In scope: the toggle and full theming of existing screens. Out of scope: per-component custom themes.
